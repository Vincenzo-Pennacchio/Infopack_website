The project.

To empower young people to become active changemakers by connecting digital innovation, social inclusion, sustainability and European green heritage through non-formal education.

The project LIGHT — Learning Inclusion & Green Heritage through Technology — supports young people in developing practical competences, attitudes and tools to promote environmental responsibility, inclusive participation and digital creativity in their communities.

This mobility is a Youth Exchange hosted in La Spezia, Italy, bringing together young people from Italy, Latvia, Greece, Romania and Spain. It focuses on the connection between green transition, digital transformation, social inclusion and cultural heritage.

Participants will explore how cultural and natural heritage can inspire climate action, responsible consumption and civic engagement — and will learn how to use digital tools, storytelling and social media to communicate meaningful messages and create youth-led campaigns with local and European impact.

The ideal participant

  • 18–25 years old (preferably 22–25+)
  • Minimum English level
  • Motivated to engage actively
  • Interested in sustainability & heritage
  • Curious about digital storytelling
  • Open to intercultural learning
  • Ready to share results back home
  • 2 fewer-opportunities per country

* First-time Erasmus+ youth have priority · gender balance recommended

Objectives

  1. i.

    Increase awareness about European green heritage and sustainable practices

    For young participants from Italy, Latvia, Greece, Romania and Spain.

  2. ii.

    Develop digital competences and media literacy

    By supporting participants in creating youth-led digital campaigns on sustainability, inclusion and cultural heritage.

  3. iii.

    Promote social inclusion and active youth participation

    A safe intercultural learning space where young people — including 2 participants with fewer opportunities per country — can cooperate and take active roles.

  4. iv.

    Strengthen teamwork, intercultural dialogue and leadership skills

    Through group challenges, creative workshops, peer learning and daily reflection.

  5. v.

    Encourage follow-up actions in local communities

    Through youth-led campaigns, dissemination activities and reusable project outputs.

Schedule of activities will be posted no later than one week before the mobility.

Important

Approval before purchase.

All travel itineraries must be approved by your national partner organisation before any ticket is purchased. Do not book or buy tickets without prior confirmation from your reference partner organisation.

From airport to La Spezia Centrale — organise your trip by train or bus/train combination after landing. Check connections carefully before buying tickets, and make sure your arrival time allows you to reach La Spezia Centrale on arrival day.

From La Spezia Centrale to the venue — the venue is outside the city, in Rocchetta di Vara / Val di Vara. The team will arrange three car transfers from La Spezia Centrale in different time slots, grouped by your arrival schedule. Share your travel details with us as soon as your tickets are purchased.

Notify the team immediately in case of:

  • Flight delay
  • Train delay
  • Missed connection
  • Change of arrival time
  • Any emergency during travel

What to bring.

November in Liguria can be cool, rainy and humid. Pack warm, waterproof, comfortable — and bring something of home for the intercultural evenings.

Mandatory

A reusable water bottle.

Let's keep LIGHT green. Water will be available throughout the activities — bring your own bottle and refill it to avoid unnecessary plastic waste.

Clothes & personal items

  • Comfortable indoor clothes
  • Warm clothes for evenings
  • Raincoat or waterproof jacket
  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Outdoor-appropriate shoes
  • Personal toiletries
  • Optional: extra towel
  • Small backpack for daily activities

Health & medicine

  • Personal medication (enough for full stay)
  • Headache & fever remedies
  • Stomach-ache relief
  • Allergy medication
  • Cold / sore throat remedies
  • EHIC card (EU participants)

Emergency kits will be available in the common rooms. The reception will always be active and reachable. Still, bring your own basics as a precaution.

Digital equipment

Each national team should bring 1–2 laptops for digital storytelling, campaign creation and group work. Bring chargers, adapters if needed, and useful digital material from your country.

Italy: Type C, F & L plugs · 230V · 50Hz.

For the intercultural evening

Each evening is dedicated to a different country. We can't reimburse what you bring, but we invite you to be creative — local food, sweets, drinks, traditional clothes, music, photos, fun facts, dances, games. Make it interactive and memorable.

Before you leave — final checklist

  • ID card or passport
  • EHIC / health insurance
  • Travel tickets & boarding passes
  • Proofs of payment for reimbursement
  • Reusable water bottle
  • Warm & rainproof clothes
  • Comfortable shoes
  • Personal medicines
  • Laptop for your national team
  • Something fun for the intercultural evening

La Spezia & Liguria.

North-western Italy, between the mountains and the Mediterranean. A gateway to the Cinque Terre, the Gulf of Poets, and the quiet green valleys inland.

La Spezia

A coastal city in Liguria, between Genoa and Tuscany. An important port and strategic point for reaching Cinque Terre, Portovenere, Lerici and the Gulf of Poets. Around La Spezia: sea views, hills, villages, museums, local markets and typical Italian cafés.

Rocchetta di Vara & Val di Vara

Val di Vara is the "green valley" of eastern Liguria — forests, small villages, agriculture, biodiversity, slower rhythms. A meaningful setting for a project on green heritage, sustainability, inclusion and digital storytelling.

This is not a city-based mobility. We will be in the countryside of the La Spezia province — come prepared, flexible and open-minded.

What to expect

  • Green landscapes
  • Small villages
  • Liguria's local traditions
  • Italian food culture
  • Possible outdoor activities
  • A peaceful atmosphere
  • Strong connection between nature, heritage & sustainability

Un po' di italiano.

A few typical Italian phrases to help you connect with locals. Don't worry about the accent — the attempt is always appreciated.

Ciao, come stai? Hello, how are you?
Io mi chiamo… My name is…
Ho bisogno di aiuto I need help
Buongiorno Good morning
Buonasera Good evening
Quando inizia l'aperitivo? When does the aperitivo start?
Grazie mille Thank you very much
Per favore Please
Scusi, parla inglese? Excuse me, do you speak English?
Non capisco I don't understand
Dov'è il bagno? Where is the bathroom?
Un caffè, per favore A coffee, please

Italian is a warm, expressive language — and even a few words go a long way. Locals in smaller Ligurian villages often speak little English, so knowing some phrases will genuinely help.

Don't be shy. Italians appreciate the effort, even if the pronunciation is creative. A smile and a buongiorno open every door.

L'aperitivo — the Italian ritual of early-evening drinks and snacks — is taken very seriously. Ask when it starts.
